# Definition of done

The acceptance checklist from the project brief (rows 1–7), from the Audit-01 correction
prompt (rows 8–16: amendment A4 and the website), from the guided-journey fix (rows 17–21) and
from the study-console redesign (rows 22–24),
with the status at hand-off and the evidence an auditor can check. "Verified" means checked by running code in this repository,
not by reading it. Status as of 2026-09-28 (BUILD-REPORT §10).

## What "done" does **not** mean here

- No foundation model has been evaluated on real data, or with its real weights at all.
  The primary research question is **unanswered** by the committed results. All TSFM rows
  are marked UNAVAILABLE with the reason.
- The committed numbers are about synthetic fixtures. They show the pipeline works and
  behaves sensibly (e.g. GARCH wins on GARCH-generated series, HAR on HAR-generated ones);
  they are not evidence about markets.
- The website has not been deployed and no one has signed in through a real Supabase
  project: both need the owner's accounts (BUILD-REPORT §10.8). Everything up to that point
  is tested (row 14, row 15).
- The terminal task pages have not been followed end to end on a real macOS, Windows or
  Linux laptop. Their expected outputs come from the code and from runs in the build
  container (BUILD-REPORT §11).
